Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2025

Introduction

PodMaster ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Audio Recordings: Audio files you record are stored locally on your device only.
  • File Names: Names you give to your recordings.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: We do NOT collect device information.
  • Usage Data: We do NOT collect analytics or usage data.
  • Location Data: We do NOT access your location.

How We Use Your Information

  • Audio Processing: Your recordings are processed entirely on your device.
  • Storage: All audio files are stored locally on your device storage.
  • No Cloud Upload: We do NOT upload your recordings to any server.

Data Storage

  • All recordings are stored in your device's local storage.
  • You have full control to delete recordings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app will delete all stored recordings.

Third-Party Services

PodMaster does NOT use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising networks.

Permissions We Request

Required Permissions:

  • RECORD_AUDIO: To record your voice and podcasts.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To save recordings to your device (Android 12 and below).
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To access and play your recordings (Android 12 and below).
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO: To access audio files (Android 13+).

Optional Permissions:

  • INTERNET: Only used if you choose to manually upload recordings to third-party platforms via your browser.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your recordings at any time
  • Delete any or all recordings
  • Export recordings to other devices
  • Uninstall the app and remove all data

Children's Privacy

PodMaster does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

Data Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • We do not transmit data to external servers
  • Your recordings remain private and under your control
